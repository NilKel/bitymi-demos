BITYMI demos

WebGPU Textured 2D surfel splatting with texture atlases baked from a neural representation.

All scenes use the same model: 3D_SH_res + --feature SV (per-surfel beta-scaled kernel, K=7 Spherical-Voronoi sites/surfel, BC7 residual atlas). On Wi-Fi the indoor scenes load in a couple seconds; the outdoor ones are larger so save them for a fast connection.

Quality

Lite atlases are baked at max_res 32 — about a quarter of HD's bundle size and friendlier to phones with tight memory or older WebGPU drivers. Pick Lite if HD failed to load. Untex skips the BC7 atlas entirely (mean SH only, lower visual quality) and is a diagnostic to confirm whether the rasterizer or the atlas is the blocker on a given device. Untex-lite uses the same untex bundle but a stripped-down render pipeline (no SV/SB/snugbox/atlas/kernel-type branches) for phones whose WebGPU driver wedges on the full shader.
