Fetch-count test: 1 to 4 atlas fetches per fragment

Test build of the WebGPU viewer. Each extra fetch samples the same atlas at another per-surfel location and blends 12% of it in, so the picture turns noisy on purpose: that is how you can see the fetches are live. The quality is meaningless; only the timing matters.

  1. Open the link for your device and wait for the scene.
  2. Fetches: N (bottom left) cycles 1, 2, 3, 4 live.
  3. Sweep 1-4 runs the offscreen, no-vsync benchmark for every count, twice and interleaved (about a minute on a phone), then shows a table you can long-press to copy. A gap between the two rounds of the same count means thermal throttling.

Source change against the viewer: fetchbench.patch. Start at a given count with &fetches=3.
